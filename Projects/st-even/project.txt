Detailed description: Candle Holder Auction 2026 I assigned myself to create a candle holder for the 2026 candle holder auction. I made a balancing holder inspired by vintage scale mechanisms. The mechanism is put together by a hammered-in brass tube in between brass plating. The metal tubes are grinded in a circular motion to create a raw texture on the surface. The concrete is molded in a custom TPU mold with added red pigment in one of the two versions. One is sold at auction for a successful price. The other is kept for private use.
