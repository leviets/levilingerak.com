Detailed description: BÖJ (Swedish for bend or curve) is a lamp in the spirit of IKEA: affordable, compact and flat-pack, to put together yourself. Strips of wood veneer are laser-cut and lock-stitched; the folded sheet supports itself and is held in shape with clips, and an LED filament runs along the stitching as the light.

The gaps are adjustable and the lengths can differ, and the same system can become a wall ornament, a ceiling chandelier or other custom shapes.
