# One sentence, shown at the top of the project page.

Detailed description: We started with a prototype: a round mug with a horse as the handle, with a horseshoe of clay as a fitting bottom. In the 3D model I changed the tube into a globe to make the hideous but fun theme even stronger. The model was 3D printed and used to make a three-part mould.

Casting took a lot of testing: I wrote down how long the slip stayed in the mould and how long it dried to get the right thickness. In the end we cast around eight mugs. A failed one did not survive the one-metre drop test. I love the joy the result shares; it made me enthusiastic to work with ceramics again.
