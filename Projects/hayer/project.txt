Detailed description: Hayer is a hexagonal cabinet with the record player on top, protected by a hexagonal dust cover. Below it sit the speakers, and a drawer swings out of the side to store records. The top opens up so the electronics inside can be reached.

I drew the dust cover as six side panels with 45-degree mitres and one top plate, glued together into one hexagon.
