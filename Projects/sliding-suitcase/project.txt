Detailed description: The suitcase has two parts: a frame and a sliding opening. Lay it down and pull the bottom out like a drawer; at the same time the top rolls open, so you can store things in both. The sliding part is made of thin slats glued onto one piece of fabric that runs in a groove around the frame, and it can be taken out completely. The handle is in two parts and only becomes one when the suitcase is closed, which keeps it shut.

Walnut for the frame and handle: a hard wood, so the handle is strong and the case does not dent easily. Beech for the sliding part. Small magnets in the base hold an interchangeable inside. Shown here as renders and technical drawings.
