Short description: A hands-on workshop about the future of vacant churches: build one together, imagine its new purpose, then vote.
# One sentence, shown at the top of the project page.

Detailed description: Thousands of churches across Europe have outlived their original purpose, but not their potential. In this workshop six people build one shared church from a table of wooden parts: towers, walls, roofs, arched doors and a graveyard. Each person then writes down what the church they built could become, pitches it to the group, and one idea wins the vote.

These choices are normally made by developers and companies, guided by regulations. The workshop asks what the general public would do without those limits, and makes people look at these buildings again. It took place in the Sint Gerarduskerk in Eindhoven, a vacant church with no set future.
