Detailed description: I tried a few ways of creating a stable material out of a beet itself by adding the juice in a concrete mix, compressing it in to block and drying it in different shapes. Most experiments somewhat worked but either the color or the aesthetics of the beet were lost. The nicest looking sample of these test was still a simple horizontally sliced slice of beet, with the downside that time took a toll on them.
