About

Making useful things with a point of view.

I’m Levi Lingerak, an Eindhoven-based designer and maker exploring the relationship between material, function and form.

My work moves across wood, metal, textiles, plastics and stoneware, with an emphasis on clear ideas and hands-on experimentation.
